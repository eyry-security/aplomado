# Bundled wordlist notice

These compact lists were curated specifically for Aplomado from common web,
API, parameter, and host naming conventions. They are original project content,
not copies of a third-party corpus, and are distributed under the repository's
MIT license.

Keep additions focused and reviewable. Prefer high-signal entries that are
useful across many authorized targets; specialized or very large corpora belong
in user-supplied lists rather than the default package.
